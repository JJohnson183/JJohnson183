## 💻 About Me

- Built and launched VolleyNet, a volleyball social app on iOS and Android, as the sole developer
- Finishing my undergraduate thesis, *Interactive Idlewild*, a Flutter + Firebase app preserving the history of Idlewild, Michigan
- System Administrator for WMU's College of Engineering and Applied Sciences
- Most interested in full-stack mobile development and backend systems

## 📂 Projects

- **[VolleyNet](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/volleynet-sports-social-app/id6779828510)**: volleyball social app with player matching, group chat, and live match scoring *(Flutter, Supabase)*
- **[Road Surface Classification](https://github.com/JJohnson183/ML_Road_Classification/tree/scratch-jordan)**: CNN built from scratch to classify road conditions from images *(PyTorch)*
- **[AI Receipt Reader](https://github.com/JJohnson183/AI_Receipt_Reader)**: LOCALHOST AI Hackathon project that turns receipt photos into structured data *(n8n, OpenAI)*
